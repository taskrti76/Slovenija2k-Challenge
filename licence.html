Kaj je naše

  • Seznam vrhov (prominenca iz LiDAR), poti do vrhov, ocene težavnosti in brezpotja, model snega, značke in oblikovanje so delo projekta. Izračunane poti in ocene so samodejne — preveri jih pred turo.
  • Značke in ozadja so narisani po prosto dostopnih fotografijah.
  • Pisave: Inter, Barlow Condensed (SIL Open Font License). Knjižnice: MapLibre GL JS (BSD-3), maplibre-contour (BSD-3). Ikone: Lucide (ISC, lucide.dev).

What is ours

  • The peak list (prominence from LiDAR), summit routes, difficulty and pathless estimates, snow model, badges and design are the project's own work. Computed routes and grades are automatic — check them before a tour.
  • Badges and backgrounds are drawn from freely available photos.
  • Fonts: Inter, Barlow Condensed (SIL Open Font License). Libraries: MapLibre GL JS (BSD-3), maplibre-contour (BSD-3). Icons: Lucide (ISC, lucide.dev).
