Zasebnost

Slovenski 2000-čaki · posodobljeno 1. 11. 2026

Stran je nekomercialen hobi projekt. Ni oglasov, ni analitike, ni sledilnih piškotkov. Shranjujemo samo, kar je nujno, da stran deluje.

Brez računa

Vse (odkljukani vrhovi, datumi in poti vzponov, narisane poti, nastavitve, jezik, velikost besedila) ostane samo v tvojem brskalniku (localStorage). Nihče drug tega ne vidi. Izbrišeš z brisanjem podatkov strani v brskalniku; »Izvozi« v seznamu vrhov naredi kopijo.

Računi nimajo e-pošte. Podatke hrani Supabase (podatkovni center v EU). Vsako noč se naredi šifrirana varnostna kopija, ki se po 30 dneh izbriše.

Tvoje pravice: v ⚙ Nastavitve → Račun lahko podatke kadarkoli izvoziš (»Izvozi moje podatke«, datoteka JSON), popraviš ali izbrišeš račun skupaj z vsemi podatki. Za drugo piši prek GitHub.

Zunanje storitve

Pri nalaganju karte in podatkov tvoj brskalnik neposredno pokliče te storitve; vidijo tvoj IP naslov in kateri del karte gledaš, ne pa tvojega računa:

  • GitHub Pages – sama stran in podatki o vremenu in snegu
  • Mapterhorn (višine terena), Esri (satelitska slika), OpenFreeMap (vode, kraji, pisave), OpenTopoMap, Waymarked Trails – slike in podatki karte
  • jsDelivr – knjižnice (MapLibre)
  • Open-Meteo (sloj »Višina snega«), Overpass / OpenStreetMap (državna meja, slapovi, če niso shranjeni)
  • Cloudflare Turnstile – preverjanje »nisem robot« ob prijavi in registraciji (samo takrat)
  • Supabase – samo, če imaš račun ali odpreš lestvico / deljeno pot

Privacy

Slovenian 2000ers · updated 1 November 2026

This site is a non-commercial hobby project. No ads, no analytics, no tracking cookies. Only what the site needs to work is stored.

Without an account

Everything (ticked peaks, dates and routes of climbs, drawn routes, settings, language, text size) stays only in your browser (localStorage). Nobody else sees it. Clear it by clearing this site's data in your browser; "Export" in the peak list makes a copy.

With an account

WhatWhyWho sees it
username, password (stored only as a hash), display name, account creation datesign-inname and display name: the leaderboard (unless you switch it off)
ticked peaks with date, route and noteto have them on all your devicesonly you; others see the list only if you turn on "Public profile"; the leaderboard shows only the number
profile settings (winter, fitness, hardest route, via ferratas)same settings on all devicesonly you
drawn routes (name, line, length, peaks on the way)saving and sharingonly you; "link": whoever has the link; "public": everyone (in the peak window)

Accounts have no email. The data is kept by Supabase (EU data centre). An encrypted backup is made every night and deleted after 30 days.

Your rights: in ⚙ Settings → Account you can export your data at any time ("Export my data", a JSON file), correct it, or delete the account with all its data. For anything else, write via GitHub.

Outside services

When the map and data load, your browser calls these services directly; they see your IP address and which part of the map you look at, not your account:

  • GitHub Pages – the site itself and the weather and snow data
  • Mapterhorn (terrain heights), Esri (satellite image), OpenFreeMap (water, places, fonts), OpenTopoMap, Waymarked Trails – map images and data
  • jsDelivr – libraries (MapLibre)
  • Open-Meteo (layer "Snow depth"), Overpass / OpenStreetMap (border, waterfalls, if not stored)
  • Cloudflare Turnstile – the "not a robot" check when signing in or up (only then)
  • Supabase – only if you have an account or open the leaderboard / a shared route
